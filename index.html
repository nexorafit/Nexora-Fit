What is NEXORA FIT?

More than choosing a workout.

Nexora Fit is an activity compatibility assessment platform designed to help users discover physical activities that may better align with who they are today.


Instead of simply asking what activity you want to do, Nexora Fit considers different aspects of your profile, readiness, health screening responses, fitness level, personality, goals, and preferences to provide a more informed starting point.

01

Find Your Fit

Assess how well a physical activity may match your current profile, fitness level, readiness, goals, and personal traits.

02

Understand Your Result

Go beyond a simple percentage. Nexora Fit explains the factors behind your result, including strengths, considerations, and flagged concerns.

03

Explore Alternatives

If an activity may not be the best match right now, explore other activities that may better align with your profile.

04

Build a Starting Plan

Turn your assessment into a simple starting direction based on your goals and preferred activity frequency.

Why NEXORA FIT?

A different way to choose activity.

✓

Multiple factors

Activity suitability is explored through more than one piece of information. Physical profile, fitness level, health screening, goals, and personal traits can all contribute to the assessment.

✓

Activity-specific assessment

Different activities can present different demands. Nexora Fit uses activity-specific questions rather than treating every activity exactly the same.

✓

Explanations, not just percentages

A compatibility score is only part of the result. Nexora Fit provides context so users can understand what may be contributing to their assessment.

✓

Alternatives and next steps

The goal is not simply to tell users what they should or should not do. Nexora Fit helps them explore other possibilities and decide where to begin.
